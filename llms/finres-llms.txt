# Finres

> Finres (finres SAS) is a French climate-risk analytics company whose platform translates
> climate hazards into economic consequences for farms, agricultural value chains and
> territories. It combines agronomic, pedological, climate and market data with
> peer-reviewed AI models. Finres publishes no public API, developer portal or
> machine-readable specification as of 2026-07-20; platform data delivery into customer
> decision systems is arranged commercially through a sales demo.

- [Contact](https://www.finres.org/contact): Contact form for the Finres team.

## Solutions

- [Diagnostic a l'exploitation](https://www.finres.org/solutions-diagnostic-exploitation): Farm-level economic and climate stress test with a tailored action plan.
- [Diagnostic territorial](https://www.finres.org/solutions-diagnostic-territorial): Custom territorial and sector-level climate/economic analyses.
- [DPEC beta](https://www.finres.org/beta): Beta program for the farm economic and climate viability diagnostic, seeded from a TelePAC parcel file plus Agreste, IPPAP and RICA reference data.
- [Sign up for the beta](https://www.finres.org/inscription): Registration form for the DPEC test program.
